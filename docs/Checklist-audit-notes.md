# Hyrule Atlas checklist audit — v1.5

Reviewed November 6, 2026 against published Nintendo Switch 2 remake guides.

The list contains **256 active checks**, retaining all **238 previous record IDs** and their checkmarks. There are 257 stored guide records; the older duplicate ranch token stays archived. Nineteen genuinely missing equipment, song and reward checks were added.

## Frog rewards and coverage

There are still **36 distinct Heart Pieces**, not 37. Both frog rewards were already present but could be hidden by requirements, View, or item filters. They are now explicitly numbered “Frogs 1 of 2” and “Frogs 2 of 2”, link to each other, and the river coverage line reports four Heart Pieces, including two frog rewards. “View all Heart Pieces here” reveals collected and locked pieces without discarding the selected timeline or region. Hidden matches are counted and explained. Existing Include locked preferences are preserved.

## Requirements and timelines

- Adult trades require the preceding trade step; transport alternatives and the final knife purchase are checked.
- The first four mask sales unlock in order. After the Bunny Hood sale, the reserve masks unlock together.
- Skulltula rewards check actual active-token totals and earlier reward claims. The archived token never increases the threshold count.
- Bowling prizes, the higher archery prize, Forest Stage capacity upgrades and adult Scarecrow activation have their linked prerequisites.
- Upgraded tools satisfy earlier tool capabilities; the Longshot can satisfy a Hookshot requirement.
- Routes requiring both timelines have a warning-marked **Young + Adult** tag, appear in both age filters, and explain each timeline’s task. A route available independently in either age retains an Either age tag.

## Filters and summary

Every region badge equals the number of checks that would be shown for that region with the current timeline, item type, search, View and Include locked settings. Only the currently selected region is replaced while counting another region.

The collapsible What’s left panel sits below the plain-text filter summary. It reports remaining checks by item type for the current region/timeline/type/search, separates available from waiting-on-requirements, and remembers its open state. It reports remaining work even when View is set to Completed.
