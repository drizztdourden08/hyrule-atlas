# Hyrule Atlas guide update — v1.2

Edition: Nintendo Switch 2 remake. Research date: November 6, 2026.

All 238 existing record IDs are retained. The app now shows 237 active entries, including exactly 100 Gold Skulltulas and 36 Heart Pieces. One extra legacy ranch description is archived with its old checkmark intact. 1014 numbered steps were written across the records, using 99 linked source pages. This is a source-based guide; it has not been independently playtested in every location.

Each expanded entry includes regional orientation, required equipment, time of day, numbered actions, targeted route notes, and a link to the relevant remake guide. Links open in the phone browser; the instructions and art remain available offline.

## Main corrections

- The castle Song of Storms grotto is anchored to Talon’s former sleeping spot at the castle-side moat, with the corner tree and colored ground plant identified. It is distinguished from the town gate, the outside-field Scrub grotto, and the fairy-fountain rock.
- Both seed-bag venue IDs keep their earlier checkmarks. Capacity advances with your existing bag rather than being assigned permanently to the opposite venue.
- Dampé’s digging reward is no longer described as a guaranteed fourth dig.
- The Deku Tree’s last token uses the remake’s burned-web route rather than a later Bombs/Boomerang return.
- Remake relocations include the Lake Hylia crawlspace, Bean Seller soil, Forest Temple Scarecrow ledge, and several dungeon tokens.
- The summit fairy gives magic and Din’s Fire; the castle fairy gives the Spin Attack. Costs and later fairy routes are included.
- The western Hyrule Field chest and moat-side Scrub now explain their adult Fairy Notes and child return visits.
- Equipment requirements no longer hide soil spiders behind Magic Beans or block the Fire Temple until you already own its Hammer.
- Published archery scores and diving timers conflict; affected entries tell you to use the target displayed in your game.

## Save compatibility

The package name, signing key, native preference file, completed-entry IDs, and milestone keys are unchanged. No old checkmark is deleted. The archived ranch ID remains in saved and exported data. The retired entry no longer inflates the completion denominator.

## Validation

The rendered app was checked at 412px and 360px widths. Automated checks cover guide coverage, numbered lists, all required icons, prerequisite cycles, the castle route, unchanged legacy completion IDs, seed-bag venue identity, saved filters, reload, JSON export and undo.
